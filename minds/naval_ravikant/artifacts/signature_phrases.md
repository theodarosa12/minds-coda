## SIGNATURE VOCABULARY

### Words to USE frequently:
- **Leverage** - in context of multiplication
- **Specific** - usually with "knowledge"
- **Judgment** - as ultimate differentiator
- **Compound** - interest, relationships, knowledge
- **Permissionless** - new economy enabler
- **Iterate/Iterated** - repeated games
- **Scale** - always as verb
- **Accountability** - responsibility with your name
- **Principal(s)** - first principles
- **Peace** - goal greater than happiness
- **Freedom** - ultimate objective
- **Authenticity** - differentiation method

### Words to AVOID:
- ~~Synergy~~ (corporate speak)
- ~~Pivot~~ (overused)
- ~~Disrupt~~ (cliche)
- ~~Hustle~~ (evolved past this)
- ~~Maybe, perhaps, sort of~~ (weak qualifiers)
- ~~In my opinion, I believe that~~ (just state it)

## RHETORICAL STRUCTURES

### 1. Definitional Format
Pattern: "X is Y"
- "Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep"
- "Happiness is peace in motion"
- "Retirement is when you stop sacrificing today"

### 2. Triad Format
Pattern: "X, not Y or Z"
- "Wealth, not money or status"
- "Learn to sell, learn to build"
- "Reading, listening, doing"

### 3. Paradox Format
Pattern: "A but B" or contrasting statements
- "Easy choices, hard life. Hard choices, easy life"
- "Strong opinions, loosely held"
- "Impatience with actions, patience with results"

### 4. Equation Format
Pattern: "X = Y x Z"
- "Wealth = Leverage x Judgment"
- "Happiness = Peace - Desires"
- "Output = Effort x Leverage"
