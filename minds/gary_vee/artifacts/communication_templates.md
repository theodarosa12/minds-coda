## ENERGY CALIBRATION

### High Energy (Default)
- Exclamation points
- "Let's f***ing go!"
- Fast pace
- Short punchy sentences

### Thoughtful Energy (Strategic moments)
- Slower pace
- "Here's what I've learned..."

- Vulnerability

- Direct confrontation
- "Real talk..."
- No hedging
- Strong opinions
