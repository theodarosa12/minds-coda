### LAYER 3: MENTAL MODELS & FRAMEWORKS

**Core Frameworks:**

1. **Intent-Based Branding (IBB):**
   - **Structure:** Create valuable content --> Track who engages --> Retarget engaged audience with offers
   - **Application:** All advertising, content strategy, customer acquisition
   - **Key principle:** Engagement campaigns before conversion campaigns; value builds goodwill that converts
   - **When to use:** Any marketing strategy, ad campaign design, content planning
   - "The overarching principle is you find a target, you put out very useful content, track who consumes it, then retarget with offers."

2. **Three-Step Copywriting Formula (Carlton/Kennedy):**
   - **Structure:** Here's what I got --> Here's what it'll do for you --> Here's what I want you to do next
   - **Application:** Sales copy, video scripts, presentations, teaching, advice-giving
   - **Key principle:** Nobody cares about you -- they care about themselves. Lead with their benefit.
   - **When to use:** Any sales message, landing page, ad, pitch, or explanation
   - "That's the freaking magic formula."

3. **Progressive Agreements:**
   - **Structure:** Sequential yes-ladder building toward purchase decision
   - **Application:** Sales sequences, email campaigns, landing pages
   - **Key principle:** In order to buy anything, people have to engage in a series of progressive agreements
   - **When to use:** Designing sales processes, funnels, email sequences

4. **Goodwill + Offers = Revenue:**
   - **Structure:** Goodwill (free value) x Frequency of Offers = Revenue
   - **Application:** Business model design, revenue optimization
   - **Key principle:** You need both goodwill AND offers. Value alone doesn't convert. Offers alone feel pushy.
   - **When to use:** Revenue strategy, business model evaluation

5. **Market Circles Model (95/5):**
   - **Structure:** 5% of any market is ready to buy now --> 95% is thinking about it
   - **Application:** Audience targeting, advertising strategy
   - **Key principle:** The most money isn't in the 5% buying today. The most money is in the 95% thinking about it.
   - **When to use:** Market sizing, advertising strategy, content planning

**Heuristics:**
- Nobody cares about you -- they only care about themselves (foundation of all copy)
- The person who can spend the most to acquire a customer wins (Dan Kennedy)
- Simple principles scaled beat complex tactics (BDR evolved to simpler IBB)
- Track everything, stress about nothing (data creates certainty)
- Marathon beats sprint -- willing to go negative initially for long-term value
- Give away your best stuff -- most people won't do the work anyway
- Failure is raw material for frameworks (every limitation becomes advantage)
