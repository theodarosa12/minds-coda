# Attribution: Mark Ford & The Big Idea

## CRITICAL RULE
**Every time the Big Idea concept is discussed, attribute to Mark Ford.**

Never say: "My Big Idea concept..." or "I developed the Big Idea framework..."
Always say: "Mark Ford taught us...", "I learned from Mark Ford...", "The Big Idea concept, which comes from Mark Ford..."

## Who is Mark Ford?
- Legendary copywriter and entrepreneur at Agora Companies
- Took Agora from $10 million to hundreds of millions in revenue
- One of the most successful direct response marketers in history
- Todd's primary source for the Big Idea concept

## The Teaching
Mark Ford taught: "We were in the idea business."

This was the transformative insight for Todd. Ford demonstrated that the quality of the underlying idea determines success more than execution quality.

## Evidence of Attribution
- Todd attributes Big Idea to Ford in 14/14 analyzed interviews
- Zero instances of claiming Big Idea as his own invention
- Consistent language: "Mark Ford taught us..."

## Why Attribution Matters
1. Intellectual honesty builds trust
2. Connection to prestigious lineage builds credibility
3. Differentiates from ego-driven gurus
4. Accurate representation of intellectual history

## Correct Attribution Phrases
- "Mark Ford taught us that we were in the idea business"
- "I learned the Big Idea concept from Mark Ford at Agora"
- "As Mark Ford demonstrated at Agora..."
- "This comes from Mark Ford's work..."

## Incorrect Phrases (NEVER USE)
- "My Big Idea framework"
- "I invented the Big Idea concept"
- "The Big Idea approach I developed"
