# The Marketing Hierarchy: Idea > Mechanism > Offer > Copy

## The Hierarchy

### Priority 1: Big Idea (40% weight)
The single, unifying concept that's intellectually interesting and emotionally compelling.
- **If broken:** STOP. Nothing else matters until this is fixed.
- **Test:** Can you explain it in 15 seconds?

### Priority 2: Unique Mechanism (25% weight)
The unique manner, method, or material that delivers the promise.
- **If broken:** Copy optimization is wasted effort.
- **Test:** Does it explain WHY and HOW differently?

### Priority 3: SIN Offer (20% weight)
Superior, Irresistible, No-brainer offer construction.
- **If broken:** Good copy can't overcome weak offer.
- **Test:** Is decision obvious?

### Priority 4: Copy Execution (15% weight)
How you articulate the above components.
- **Only fix:** After 1-3 are solid.
- **Reality:** Most "copy problems" are actually idea problems.

## Critical Rule
**"Button colors are noise, idea is signal."**

Never optimize downstream while upstream is broken. Fixing copy when the idea is weak is like polishing a cracked foundation.

## Diagnosis Process
When something isn't working:
1. First: Check Big Idea (15-second test)
2. Second: Check Unique Mechanism (believability)
3. Third: Check Offer (SIN criteria)
4. Only then: Examine copy execution

## Why This Order?
- Upstream problems propagate downstream
- Optimization below the problem wastes resources
- Most people try to fix the wrong level
- 90% of "copy problems" are really idea problems

## Application
- **Campaign diagnosis:** Start from top
- **Resource allocation:** Invest most at top
- **Optimization priority:** Fix highest broken level first
- **Teaching:** Always redirect to appropriate level
