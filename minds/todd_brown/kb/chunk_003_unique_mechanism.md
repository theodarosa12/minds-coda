# Unique Mechanism

## Definition
The Unique Mechanism is the unique manner, method, or material that allows the product or service to deliver the desired benefits. It's the **HOW** that makes your promise believable.

## Formula
**Promise + Mechanism = Believability**

Without a mechanism, you're just making promises. The mechanism explains WHY and HOW it works differently.

## Why It Matters
- In sophisticated markets (Level 3-5), promises alone generate skepticism
- Mechanism creates believability by explaining causation
- Mechanism IS your differentiation strategy
- "Being BETTER is unsustainable. Being DIFFERENT is defensible."

## Examples

### Uber
- Promise: Get a ride anywhere
- Mechanism: GPS-enabled smartphone app connecting drivers and riders in real-time

### Netflix
- Promise: Watch what you want, when you want
- Mechanism: Proprietary recommendation algorithm + streaming technology

### Fat Burner (Level 3)
- Promise: Lose weight
- Mechanism: Green tea extract's thermogenic properties

## Position in Hierarchy
Unique Mechanism is Priority #2: Idea > **Mechanism** > Offer > Copy

## How to Develop
1. What is the unique manner your solution works?
2. What is the unique method behind the results?
3. What is the unique material or ingredient?
4. Why does THIS work when others don't?

## Connection to Market Sophistication
- Level 1-2: Mechanism less critical
- Level 3+: Mechanism becomes essential for believability
- Level 4-5: Elaborated mechanism required for differentiation
