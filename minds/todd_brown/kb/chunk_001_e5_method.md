# E5 Method Overview

## Definition
The E5 Method is a five-stage process for engineering profitable, predictable customer acquisition campaigns. "Engineering" is intentional - this is systematic, not artistic.

## The Five Stages

### Stage 1: EXAMINE
**Focus:** Research prospects, products, competition
**Output:** Market sophistication understanding, prospect insight
**Key Question:** "What level is this market at?"

### Stage 2: ENGINEER
**Focus:** Build Big Idea, Unique Mechanism, SIN Offer
**Output:** Campaign components
**Key Question:** "Do we have a Big Idea that can be explained in 15 seconds?"
**Gate:** Nothing proceeds without validated Big Idea

### Stage 3: EVALUATE
**Focus:** Test with Minimum Viable Funnel (MVF)
**Output:** Validation data
**Key Question:** "Does the data validate our approach?"
**Gate:** Go/no-go decision based on MVF results

### Stage 4: ENHANCE
**Focus:** Optimize proven elements
**Output:** Full campaign
**Key Question:** "How do we improve what's working?"
**Note:** Only happens AFTER Stage 3 validation

### Stage 5: EXPAND
**Focus:** Scale through media buying
**Output:** Growth
**Key Question:** "Unit economics support scaling?"

## Critical Rules
- **Sequential execution is mandatory** - you cannot skip stages
- **Stage 2 is where the Big Idea gets developed** - this is the most critical stage
- **Stage 3 validation must happen BEFORE Stage 4 optimization**
- **Never scale (Stage 5) without validation (Stage 3)**

## Origin
E5 Method is Todd Brown's synthesis of principles learned from Mark Ford, Eugene Schwartz, and others into an operational framework.

## When to Apply
Any marketing campaign development, customer acquisition strategy, funnel building.
