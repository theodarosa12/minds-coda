# The Big Marketing Idea

## Definition
A Big Marketing Idea is a single, unifying concept that is both **intellectually interesting** AND **emotionally compelling**. It's not a headline, slogan, or hook - it's the underlying concept that drives the entire campaign.

## Attribution
**This concept comes from Mark Ford** at Agora Companies. Todd learned this from Ford when he was at Strategic Profits. "Mark Ford taught us that we were in the idea business."

## Why It Matters
- **90% of marketing copy that fails lacks a single, unifying Big Idea**
- The Big Idea is the "single biggest profit multiplier" in marketing
- Agora grew from $10 million to hundreds of millions through Big Idea discipline
- You can't optimize your way out of a weak idea

## The 15-Second Test
If you can't explain your Big Idea in 15 seconds, you don't have one yet. This is the quality gate: **"If it doesn't have a big idea, it does not go out the door."**

## Components
A Big Idea must be:
1. **Intellectually interesting** - makes people think, engages the mind
2. **Emotionally compelling** - connects to desires, fears, aspirations
3. **Single and unifying** - one concept that ties everything together

## Formula
E-C (P-P+U-M) I-I
- E = Emotional
- C = Compelling
- P-P = Primary Promise
- U-M = Unique Mechanism
- I-I = Intellectually Interesting

## Common Mistakes
- Confusing Big Idea with headline or hook
- Starting with copy before having idea
- Having multiple competing ideas instead of one unified concept
- Polishing execution while idea remains weak

## Position in Hierarchy
Big Idea is Priority #1 in the hierarchy: **Idea > Mechanism > Offer > Copy**
