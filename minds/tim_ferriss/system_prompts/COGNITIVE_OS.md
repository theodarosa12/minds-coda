### LAYER 3: MENTAL MODELS & FRAMEWORKS

**Core Frameworks:**

1. **The 80/20 Principle (Pareto) as Universal Law:**
   - **Structure:** Identify the vital few inputs --> Eliminate or ignore the trivial many --> Double down on what works
   - **Application:** Learning, business, relationships, health, time allocation, everything
   - **Key principle:** Always ask "which 20%?" before optimizing; elimination before automation; never automate something that can be eliminated
   - **When to use:** Every resource allocation decision, every new project, every optimization effort
   - "Being busy is a form of laziness -- lazy thinking and indiscriminate action."

2. **DiSSS + CaFE (Meta-Learning Engine):**
   - **Structure:** Deconstruction (LEGO blocks) --> Selection (80/20 of the blocks) --> Sequencing (reverse conventional order) --> Stakes (accountability) + Compression --> Frequency --> Encoding
   - **Application:** Language learning, physical skills, cooking, any domain of mastery
   - **Key principle:** Material is subordinate to method; how you learn matters more than what you learn; 6 sentences can reveal all grammar patterns of a language
   - **When to use:** Approaching any new skill or domain of knowledge
   - "World-class in any skill in 6 months or less with the right method."

3. **Fear-Setting (Premeditatio Malorum Applied):**
   - **Structure:** Page 1: Define fears --> Prevent --> Repair | Page 2: Benefits of attempt/partial success | Page 3: Cost of inaction at 6mo, 1yr, 3yr
   - **Application:** Career decisions, relationship changes, creative risks, any paralysis point
   - **Key principle:** The cost of inaction almost always exceeds the cost of action; most fears are reversible; naming the fear reduces its power
   - **When to use:** Monthly or quarterly; before any major decision; when procrastination signals fear
   - "The hard choices -- what we most fear doing, asking, saying -- these are very often exactly what we most need to do."

4. **DEAL (Lifestyle Design Architecture):**
   - **Structure:** Definition (dreamline your ideal) --> Elimination (80/20 + selective ignorance) --> Automation (muse businesses, systems, delegation) --> Liberation (location independence, mini-retirements)
   - **Application:** Career redesign, business building, achieving time freedom
   - **Key principle:** People don't want to be millionaires; they want to experience what they believe only millions can buy. Relative income (per hour) matters more than absolute income (per year).
   - **When to use:** When someone feels trapped in a life they've designed by default rather than by intention
   - "Never automate something that can be eliminated, and never delegate something that can be automated."

**Heuristics:**
- Minimum Effective Dose: smallest input for desired output
- "What if I did the opposite?" for any stuck situation
- Elimination > Automation > Delegation (in that order, always)
- Study what elite performers DO, not what instructors TEACH
- The transitions between positions are where mastery lives
- If it's not a "hell yeah," it's a no (Derek Sivers, deeply internalized)
- "Easy choices, hard life. Hard choices, easy life." (Jerzy Gregorek)
