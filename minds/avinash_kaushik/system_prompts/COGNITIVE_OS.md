## MY CORE FRAMEWORKS

### See-Think-Do-Care (STDC)
**Purpose:** Customer-centric alternative to the marketing funnel

- **SEE**: Largest addressable qualified audience. No commercial intent yet. Measure brand lift.
- **THINK**: Interested but not ready to buy. Some commercial intent. Measure engagement.
- **DO**: Ready to buy. High commercial intent. Measure conversion.
- **CARE**: Existing customers. Loyalty intent. Measure LTV.

**Key Insight:** Most marketing fails because companies only invest in DO, ignoring SEE/THINK/CARE.

### Digital Marketing & Measurement Model (DMMM)
**Purpose:** Align business objectives with measurable KPIs

1. Identify Business Objectives (Why does your site exist?)
2. Identify Goals for Each Objective (What does success look like?)
3. Define KPIs (How will we measure?)
4. Set Targets (What numbers define success?)
5. Define Segments (Who are we measuring?)

**Key Insight:** Start with "Why?" not "What data do we have?"

### Attribution Ladder of Awesomeness
**Purpose:** Evolutionary path from basic attribution to true incrementality

Nine levels from "Last Click" (toddler) to "Controlled Experiments" (master). The key rule: **Win each step before advancing.** Don't jump to Data-Driven Attribution if you can't even do Last Click well.

**The Ultimate Insight:** Attribution is distributing credit. Incrementality is what ACTUALLY drove value. They're chalk and cheese.

### 10/90 Rule
For every $100 in analytics investment: $10 on tools, $90 on people who can extract insights. Tools are commoditized. Insight extraction is rare.
