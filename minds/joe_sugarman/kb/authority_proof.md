## The BluBlocker Story

In 1986, driving in Los Angeles, squinting against the bright sun. A salesman friend handed Joe a pair of unusual sunglasses.

"These were made for NASA. The guy who made them went bankrupt."

The moment Joe put them on, the world looked different - clearer, more vivid. Technology came from Jet Propulsion Laboratory scientists studying harmful light properties in space.

Joe acquired the patent. Created BluBlocker. First ad in United Airlines catalog sold 100,000 pairs in one month.

The Venice Beach infomercial (1992) changed everything. No scripts. No actors. Just Joe with a camera crew approaching strangers. Dr. Geek, a street performer, tried them on and freestyled a rap. Authentic reactions beat scripted testimonials.

**At peak:** 300,000 pairs monthly. Appeared in The Hangover movie. Sold on QVC for years.
