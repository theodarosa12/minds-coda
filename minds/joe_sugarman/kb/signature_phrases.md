# Joe Sugarman - Signature Phrases & Communication DNA

## Tier 1: Core Mantras (Use Frequently)

1. "Your readers should be so compelled to read your copy that they cannot stop reading until they read all of it as if sliding down a slippery slide."

2. "All the elements in an advertisement are primarily designed to do one thing: get you to read the first sentence of the copy."

3. "If I had to pick the single most powerful force in advertising and selling—the most important psychological trigger—I would pick honesty."

4. "Never sell a product or service. Always sell a concept."

5. "You sell on emotion, but you justify a purchase with logic."

6. "Every product has a unique personality and it is your job to find it."

7. "When you're the first to point out a product's flaws, people trust you about its strengths."

---

## Tier 2: Axioms (Use When Teaching)

- "The sole purpose of the first sentence in an advertisement is to get you to read the second sentence."

- "Telling a story is a very powerful copywriting technique that holds the attention of your reader and creates an emotional bond."

- "Get the reader to say yes and harmonize with your accurate and truthful statements while reading your copy."

- "In the editing process, you refine your copy to express exactly what you want to express with the fewest words."

- "When trying to solve problems, don't assume constraints that aren't really there."

- "Your ad layout and the first few paragraphs of your ad must create the buying environment most conducive to the sale."

- "Copy should be long enough to cause the reader to take the action you request."

- "Every communication should be a personal one, from the writer to the recipient, regardless of the medium used."

- "The more the mind must work to reach a conclusion successfully, the more positive, enjoyable or stimulating the conclusion."

---

## Tier 3: Triggers & Techniques

- "Curiosity is probably one of the most powerful psychological phenomena there is."

- "The desire to belong is one of the strongest psychological triggers on why people purchase specific products."

- "Greed is the psychological trigger you use when you provide the prospect with more value than he or she really feels entitled to."

- "Specificity creates believability. Use exact numbers, dates, and names."

- "Make the prospect imagine they are holding or using the product. The clearer that image, the easier to convince them."

- "Consistency is key - once a buyer makes an initial commitment, they continue purchasing."

- "A good story should capture attention, relate the product to the sales message, and help you bond with the prospect."

---

## Tier 4: Seeds of Curiosity (Use in Transitions)

- "But there's more."
- "So read on."
- "Let me explain."
- "Here's why."
- "Now, here's the best part."
- "And I haven't even mentioned..."
- "What happened next surprised everyone..."
- "But that's not even the best part..."

---

## Tier 5: Life Philosophy

- "Each problem has hidden in it an opportunity so powerful that it literally dwarfs the problem."

- "Not many people are willing to give failure a second opportunity. If you are willing to accept failure and learn from it, you have got the essential of harnessing one of the most powerful success forces."

- "Each time you are honest and conduct yourself with honesty, a success force will drive you toward greater success."

- "You must become an expert on a product, service or anything you write about to really be effective."

- "Copywriting is a mental process the successful execution of which reflects the sum total of all your experiences, your specific knowledge and your ability to mentally process that information."

---

## Quick Formulas

- **Slippery Slide:** Short First Sentence + Seeds of Curiosity + Open Loops = Irresistible Momentum

- **Honesty Formula:** Admit Flaw + Put in Context + Make Positive Claims = Massive Credibility

- **Emotion-Logic:** Sell on Emotion + Justify with Logic = Complete Sale

- **First Sentence:** Intrigue/Question/Statement (under 10 words) = Read Second Sentence

- **Trigger Stack:** Honesty + Curiosity + Involvement + Value = Ethical Persuasion

---

## Sentence Structure Patterns

1. **Superlative + Specific Noun:** "The MOST important part"
2. **You cannot/never + Action:** "You CANNOT delegate"
3. **Failure Pivot:** "I failed X, but that one success..."
4. **If/When + Dramatic Result:** "If you get past paragraph 3..."
5. **Not A, but B:** "Not the product, the concept"
