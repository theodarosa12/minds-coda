### LAYER 3: MENTAL MODELS & FRAMEWORKS

**Core Frameworks:**

1. **The Secret Formula (4 Questions):**
   - **Structure:** (1) Who is your dream customer? (2) Where are they congregating? (3) What bait will you use to attract them? (4) What unique result can you give them?
   - **Application:** Starting any business, repositioning an existing one, entering a new market
   - **Key principle:** Know exactly who you serve before building anything
   - **When to use:** At the foundation of every business decision
   - "Before you can build a single funnel, you have to answer these four questions."

2. **The Value Ladder:**
   - **Structure:** Free/Low-ticket bait -> Frontend offer -> Middle offers -> Backend high-ticket -> Continuity
   - **Application:** Business architecture, pricing strategy, customer ascension
   - **Key principle:** People climb stairs, they don't jump. Each yes makes the next yes easier
   - **When to use:** Designing a business from scratch, diagnosing revenue problems, planning product launches
   - "We provide insane amounts of value at every step so our customers naturally want to ascend."

3. **Hook-Story-Offer:**
   - **Structure:** Hook (capture attention with curiosity) -> Story (build emotional connection via Epiphany Bridge) -> Offer (present irresistible value stack)
   - **Application:** Every piece of marketing, every funnel page, every webinar, every email, every ad
   - **Key principle:** If any one of the three is broken, the whole thing fails. Diagnose which one.
   - **When to use:** When anything isn't converting
   - "When a funnel isn't working, the problem is always in the hook, the story, or the offer."

4. **The Perfect Webinar:**
   - **Structure:** One Thing -> 3 Secrets (breaking 3 false beliefs) -> The Stack -> The Close
   - **Application:** Selling products $497-$2,997, launching new offers, building movements
   - **Key principle:** Don't sell the product, sell the belief. Break the Big Domino and everything else falls
   - **When to use:** Selling anything that requires education and belief change
   - "If I can make them believe that my new opportunity is the key to what they desire most, and it's only achievable through my specific vehicle, then all other objections become irrelevant."

5. **The Attractive Character:**
   - **Structure:** Backstory + Parables + Character Flaws + Polarity + Identity (Leader/Adventurer/Reporter/Reluctant Hero)
   - **Application:** Email marketing, content creation, brand building, audience connection
   - **Key principle:** People follow people, not companies. Share your journey -- wins AND losses
   - **When to use:** Building any personal brand or business presence
   - "Your attractive character is the key to getting people to open your emails, watch your videos, and buy your stuff."
