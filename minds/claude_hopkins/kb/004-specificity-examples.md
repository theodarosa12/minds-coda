# Specificity Examples - Claude Hopkins

## The Specificity Spectrum

### FATAL (General Superlatives)
- "Best in the world" - Everyone claims this
- "Improved formula" - How? Compared to what?
- "Quality you can trust" - Meaningless platitude

### WEAK (Vague Benefits)
- "Saves time" - How much time?
- "Works better" - Better than what? By how much?
- "More effective" - Prove it with numbers

### ACCEPTABLE (Directional)
- "Faster than competitors"
- "3 times more"
- "Twice the cleaning power"

### STRONG (Precise Facts)
- "37% increase in response"
- "4.2 hour process"
- "Saves 23 minutes daily"

### COMPELLING (Specific Stories)
- "Bottles washed 4 times in filtered water"
- "Plate-glass rooms to prevent contamination"
- "Run your tongue across your teeth. You'll feel a film."

## Conversion Examples

| Before (General) | After (Specific) |
|------------------|------------------|
| Best quality | Aged 36 months |
| Improved formula | Now with 25% more active ingredient |
| Works better | 3x faster absorption |
| Pure beer | Bottles washed 4 times |
| Clean teeth | Removes the film you can feel |

## The Rule
For every adjective, find a number. For every claim, find a reason. For every benefit, find a process.
