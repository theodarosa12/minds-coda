# Deep Research Protocol - Claude Hopkins

## The Research Depth Ladder

1. **Surface** - Read marketing materials (INSUFFICIENT)
2. **Secondary** - Industry publications (INSUFFICIENT)
3. **Primary** - Interview experts (MINIMUM)
4. **Immersion** - Visit production, observe process (STANDARD)
5. **Mastery** - Can explain simply to stranger (TARGET)

## Research Activities

### Physical Investigation
- Factory/production facility visits
- Observe manufacturing process
- Talk to workers on the line
- See quality control in action
- Document specific details

### Expert Consultation
- Interview company scientists/engineers
- Talk to industry experts
- Consult technical literature
- Understand competitive landscape

### Customer Understanding
- Understand customer problems
- Know customer language
- Identify decision factors
- Map customer journey

## Time Investment Guidelines
- New product category: 2-4 weeks
- Familiar category: 1-2 weeks
- Campaign update: 2-5 days
- NEVER: Less than 2 days for major campaign

## Quality Gate
"Can I explain this product simply to a stranger?"
- YES = Ready to write
- NO = More research needed

## The Schlitz Example
"I went through that brewery." Found they washed bottles 4 times in filtered water, used plate-glass rooms, filtered air. Every brewery did this - Hopkins was first to TELL them.
