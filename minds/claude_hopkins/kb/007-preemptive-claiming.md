# Preemptive Claiming Strategy - Claude Hopkins

## The Core Insight

You don't have to be different - you have to be FIRST to articulate what everyone does.

The brewery that claims "pure" first owns purity, even if all breweries are equally pure. First to articulate wins the perception; followers are seen as copycats.

## Strategic Logic

1. Most products in a category have similar qualities
2. Most of these qualities are never communicated
3. The first to articulate a quality OWNS that quality
4. Find universal truths and claim them first

## Discovery Process

1. **Research** what ALL competitors in category do
2. **Identify** quality standards that are UNIVERSAL
3. **Find** which universal truths have NOT been claimed
4. **Articulate** the universal truth FIRST with specificity
5. **Own** the perception; competitors become "me too"

## Case Studies

### Schlitz Beer
- **Universal Truth**: All breweries use clean processes
- **Preemptive Claim**: "Bottles washed 4 times, plate-glass rooms, filtered air"
- **Result**: Schlitz owned 'purity' despite equal purity elsewhere
- Went from fifth to first in market share

### Quaker Oats
- **Universal Truth**: All puffed cereals use similar process
- **Preemptive Claim**: "Shot from guns" (dramatic naming)
- **Result**: Quaker owned the process perception

## Rules of Application

1. **Research competitors before claiming** - Know what's already claimed
2. **Universal truths are claimable** - If everyone does it but no one says it, it's yours
3. **Specificity protects claims** - Specific process claims are harder to copy
4. **Speed matters** - Claim before competitors do
