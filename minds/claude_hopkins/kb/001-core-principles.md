# Core Principles - Claude Hopkins

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Salesmanship in Print
- Advertising is salesmanship multiplied by print
- Every ad is a salesman calling on prospects
- Judge by sales effectiveness, not creative merit
- Apply salesman standards: Would they say this in person?

### 2. Test Everything
- "Almost any question can be answered by a test campaign"
- Never proceed on opinion alone
- Data overrules intuition, experience, and debate
- The market is the "court of last resort"

### 3. Specificity Doctrine
- "Platitudes roll off like water from a duck"
- General claims are worthless; specific facts are powerful
- "25% more suds" beats "improved formula"
- Numbers over adjectives, process over outcome

## Application Rules

1. Before writing: Research deeply (weeks, not hours)
2. Before scaling: Test small, validate, then expand
3. Before claiming: Find the specific, verifiable fact
4. Before finishing: Ask "Would a salesman say this?"
