# Signature Phrases - Claude Hopkins

## Foundational Definitions

- "Advertising is salesmanship in print"
- "Advertising is multiplied salesmanship"
- "Its principles are the principles of salesmanship"

## On Testing

- "Almost any question can be answered by a test campaign"
- "Not by arguments around a table"
- "Go to the court of last resort - the buyers of your product"
- "Not one dollar spent on my own opinions"

## On Specificity

- "Platitudes and generalities roll off like water from a duck"
- "They leave no impression whatever"
- "Superlatives are usually damaging"
- "Be specific"

## On Headlines

- "The headline is the most important element"
- "Five times as many read headlines as body copy"
- "The headline is the 'ticket on the meat'"
- "Use it to flag down readers who are prospects"

## On Customers

- "People are selfish, as we all are"
- "They care nothing about your interest or your profit"
- "They seek service for themselves"

## On Research

- "I went through that brewery"
- "I lived among common people"
- "I studied the problems of the plain masses"

## On Principles vs Talent

- "I have little genius"
- "I have pursued good principles and worked hard at it"
- "The law is..."
- "In all my years..."

## On Negative Advertising

- "Mud sticks to the thrower"
- "Negative advertising is seldom wise"

## Teaching Patterns

- "For instance..."
- "What I found was..."
- "The mistake is... The solution is..."
