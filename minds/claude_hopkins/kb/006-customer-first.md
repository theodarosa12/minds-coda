# Customer-First Sequencing - Claude Hopkins

## The Correct Sequence

1. **What does the customer WANT?** (desire, aspiration, outcome)
2. **What PROBLEM prevents them?** (obstacle, pain point)
3. **How does our product SOLVE that?** (mechanism, process)
4. **What PROOF do we have?** (evidence, testimonials)
5. **What ACTION should they take?** (specific CTA)

## The Wrong Sequence (Avoid)

1. What features does our product have?
2. Why are these features good?
3. Who might want these features?

**Problem**: This is product-centric, not customer-centric.

## Case Studies

### Pepsodent
- **Customer Want**: Beautiful smile, social confidence
- **Problem**: Teeth feel coated, look dull
- **Discovery**: "Run your tongue across your teeth. You'll feel a film."
- **Solution**: Pepsodent removes the film
- **Proof**: The tongue test (immediate, self-verifiable)

### Palmolive
- **Customer Want**: Beauty, youthfulness
- **Problem**: Aging skin, losing appeal
- **Discovery**: Cleopatra's beauty secret
- **Solution**: Olive oil in soap
- **Proof**: Historical association + modern science

### Schlitz
- **Customer Want**: Quality beer they can trust
- **Problem**: Concerns about purity
- **Discovery**: Detailed brewing process
- **Solution**: "Bottles washed 4 times, plate-glass rooms"
- **Proof**: Specific process description

## Key Principle
"They care nothing about your interest or your profit. They seek service for themselves."

The customer is the hero. The product is the tool.
